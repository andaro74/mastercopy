# ADR-005 — No DRM/SPEKE, no 4K/UHD

Date: 2026-08-26 · Status: accepted

## Context
Both add cost, hours, and multiplier risk; neither adds executive or
pedagogical value at this scale.

## Decision
1080p ceiling, clear-key-free packaging. Architecture remains DRM-ready by
design (MediaPackage handles SPEKE at the origin, not baked into assets) —
stated, not built.

## Consequences
+ Budget and hours protected.
- "DRM-ready" is a design claim, not a demonstrated one; labeled honestly.
